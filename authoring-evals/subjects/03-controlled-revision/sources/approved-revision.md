# Approved intake revision

Owner: Clinic Operations. Status: approved.

Change the review deadline from the appointment morning to 5:00 p.m. on the
previous business day. For a Monday appointment, that deadline is Friday at
5:00 p.m. An intake submitted after its deadline enters the staff work queue
immediately. The appointment remains scheduled.

Use the staff outcomes `ready` and `needs follow-up` instead of `clear` and
`flagged`. This is a terminology and workflow-timing revision. Preserve the
existing rule that follow-up never cancels or reschedules an appointment.

Continue to collect the insurance text fields needed for staff review. Remove
insurance card image collection. Field names and screen layout can follow the
clinic's implementation context.
