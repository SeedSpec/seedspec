# Patient Intake

New patients complete intake before arriving, so front desk staff can resolve
problems in advance instead of at the counter. Patients can use a phone.

The intake collects patient details, insurance text fields, and an image of the
insurance card. Staff review each submission on the morning of the appointment
and mark it clear or flagged.

A flagged intake never cancels or reschedules the appointment. Staff follow up
with the patient while the appointment remains scheduled. Insurance information
is collected for staff review; the workflow does not verify payer eligibility.
