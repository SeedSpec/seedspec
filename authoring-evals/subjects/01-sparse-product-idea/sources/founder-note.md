# Founder note: tool library

I want a simple way for neighbors to lend tools to each other without deposits
or fees.

Someone lists a tool they own. A neighbor asks to borrow it. The owner approves
or declines. They arrange the handoff themselves, and the tool comes back.

Only people in the same building can see each other's tools. I have not worked
out what to do when a tool is already lent out and another person asks for it.

This is for lending between neighbors. It is not a public marketplace, a
delivery service, or a way to charge for tools.
