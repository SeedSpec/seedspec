# Approved information-handling policy

- Status: approved by the security owner.
- Authority: governs delivery channels and exposure of project information.

Weekly summaries that identify stalled work or pending decisions must use the
authenticated team channel. They must not be sent as email or email attachments.

Summaries should expose only the stalled item, responsible owner, decision title,
and requested action. Full active-work inventories and complete discussion
transcripts are not required by this policy.
